FAQ

What you might be wondering

Do you replace our recruiters?

No. We do the first pass – texting, screening and confirming – so your recruiters spend their day placing people, not chasing replies.

Whose number do the texts come from?

Yours. We work inside the texting tool you already use, with the consent your applicants already gave, and we honor every STOP.

Do we need to buy new software?

No. We work in the ATS and texting tool you already pay for. You get a shortlist each weekday and a report each week.

What counts as a start?

A worker from our shortlist who shows up and completes their first shift. That is the only thing the per-start fee is charged on.

When does the shortlist arrive?

Before your recruiters’ workday begins, every weekday, with each worker’s answers on shift, ride and certifications.

Can we try it before paying a monthly fee?

Yes. The 30-day pilot runs on one branch with no monthly fee. You only pay for workers who start.
